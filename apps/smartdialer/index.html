Privacy approach

Your information stays under your control.

SmartDialer is designed primarily to work with information available on your Android device. Contacts, call history, messages, and calendar information are processed locally for application functionality.

Core commitments

  • No selling of personal information
  • No advertising, third-party analytics, or user-tracking SDKs in the current version
  • Permission-controlled access to sensitive Android features
  • Clear documentation of local storage, retention, and deletion
