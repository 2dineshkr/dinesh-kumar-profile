Product direction

More than a list of recent calls.

The project starts from a simple question: how can everyday communication make follow-up easier rather than creating another disconnected task?

01Communicate

Calls, messages & contacts

Bring essential communication workflows into a focused, familiar Android experience.

02Organize

Reminders & events

Explore lightweight ways to connect conversations with the things that need attention next.

03Respect

Privacy by design

Treat sensitive permissions, local data, and clear user expectations as core product decisions.

Why I am building it

Product engineering beyond the backend.

SmartDialer is a hands-on opportunity to take a product from an initial problem through interaction design, Android implementation, testing, privacy review, release preparation, and iteration.

Current priorities

  • Validate the smallest useful set of connected workflows
  • Keep navigation and information architecture easy to understand
  • Document every sensitive permission and its purpose
  • Align the release build, privacy policy, and Play Data Safety declaration

Release status

SmartDialer is not presented as a released product yet. Screenshots, supported Android versions, and installation links will be published when they accurately represent a tested build.
