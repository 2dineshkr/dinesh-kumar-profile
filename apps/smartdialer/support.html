Frequently asked questions

Using SmartDialer

SmartDialer depends on Android permissions and roles to provide the features you choose to use.

Why does SmartDialer request access to contacts, calls, or messages?

These permissions support caller identification, contact search, call history, phone functionality, and messaging features. SmartDialer requests access only when it is required for the corresponding functionality.

Why might SmartDialer request the default Phone or SMS role?

Android restricts certain call and messaging capabilities to the application holding the relevant default role. Role-restricted access stops when SmartDialer no longer holds that role.

Does SmartDialer upload my contacts or messages?

SmartDialer processes contacts, call history, messages, and calendar information locally on your device. It does not operate a backend that receives these datasets.

How can I change permissions?

Open Android Settings, select Apps, choose SmartDialer, and open Permissions. Disabling a permission may prevent its related feature from working.

How can I remove SmartDialer data?

Use the available controls to remove supported messages, tasks, notes, events, and conferences. You can also clear SmartDialer’s app data from Android Settings or uninstall the application. Records held by Android system providers remain subject to their own controls.

What should I try if a feature is not working?

Confirm that SmartDialer has the required permission or default role, restart the application, and make sure Android and SmartDialer are up to date. Some capabilities may vary by Android version or device manufacturer.

Contact

Still need assistance?

Use the developer contact information published on SmartDialer’s official Google Play Store listing. Include your Android version, device model, and a description of the issue, but do not send private message content or other sensitive information.
