SmartDialer Privacy Policy

SmartDialer is a phone, messaging, contacts, and productivity application designed to help users manage calls, SMS messages, contacts, reminders, tasks, and calendar-related activities.

This policy explains what information SmartDialer may access, why that access is required, and how the information is handled.

1. Information SmartDialer accesses

Depending on the features you choose to use and the permissions you grant, SmartDialer may access certain information stored on your Android device.

Contacts

Used to display contact information, identify callers, search contacts, and provide contact-related features.

Call logs

Used for recent calls, call history, summaries, and call-related functionality.

SMS & MMS

Used when SmartDialer has the appropriate or default messaging role to display, receive, send, and manage messages.

Calendar

Used when you choose calendar features to display relevant information or create events requested by you.

Camera

May be used for supported functionality that requires camera access, such as supported video-call features.

Notifications

Used for calls, messages, reminders, tasks, events, and other relevant application activity.

2. How information is used

Information accessed by SmartDialer is used to provide features requested by you, including:

  • Displaying contacts and caller information
  • Displaying and managing call history
  • Initiating phone calls
  • Displaying, receiving, sending, and managing SMS messages
  • Providing reminders and notifications
  • Displaying and creating calendar events
  • Supporting call and messaging productivity features

3. Local data processing

SmartDialer processes contacts, phone numbers, call history, messages, calendar information, call rules, tasks, notes, conferences, and message-derived intelligence locally on your device. SMS parsing, categorization, and badges are generated on-device. SmartDialer does not operate a backend that receives these datasets.

Contacts, call logs, SMS, and calendar records normally remain in the Android system providers that manage them. A calendar account configured on your device may independently synchronize calendar data according to that provider’s settings and privacy practices; SmartDialer does not perform that synchronization.

Information stored by SmartDialer

SmartDialer may store user-created tasks, notes, call-linked metadata, favorites, contact groups and rules, scheduled conferences and participant details, message marks or reactions, and limited notification state in app-private storage. SMS content may be cached temporarily on the device while an active message notification is being managed.

Retention and deletion

Incoming-call attempt history is retained for approximately 31 minutes and is limited to 100 entries. Post-call state is limited to 250 entries. Other user-created information remains until you delete it using available SmartDialer controls, clear SmartDialer’s app data, or uninstall SmartDialer. Records held by Android system providers remain subject to their own deletion and retention controls.

You can revoke permissions in Android settings and remove supported messages, tasks, notes, events, and conferences using their available controls. Sensitive SmartDialer app data is excluded from Android cloud and device-transfer backups; only non-sensitive display and interaction settings are eligible for backup.

SmartDialer does not use your contacts, call logs, SMS messages, or calendar information for advertising, marketing, analytics, or profiling.

4. Data sharing

SmartDialer does not sell your personal information.

Information may leave the application when you explicitly initiate an action requiring another application or service, such as opening a web link, making a phone call, composing an email or SMS, opening a map or payment link, sharing content, or opening a supported third-party messaging application. The receiving application or service handles that information under its own privacy practices. SmartDialer does not send this information to external services in the background.

5. Advertising, analytics, and tracking

The current version of SmartDialer does not include advertising, third-party analytics, or user-tracking SDKs.

6. Permissions

SmartDialer requests Android permissions only when they are required for application functionality. Depending on the features you use, these permissions may include access to contacts, call logs, phone functionality, SMS, camera, notifications, and calendar information.

You can manage application permissions through your Android device settings. Disabling a permission may prevent the corresponding SmartDialer feature from working.

7. Default Phone and SMS roles

Some SmartDialer functionality requires the application to hold an appropriate Android default role, such as the default Phone or SMS application role.

SmartDialer requests permissions associated with these roles only when required for the corresponding functionality. Access to role-restricted information stops when SmartDialer no longer holds the required role.

8. Data security

SmartDialer is designed to minimize unnecessary access to personal information and to use Android’s permission and application security mechanisms to protect access to device information.

You should also keep your Android device protected and up to date to help safeguard information stored on the device.

9. Younger users and families

SmartDialer is a general-purpose dialer and productivity application intended for a broad audience aged 9 and older, including students and professionals. It is designed to make everyday communication and organization easier through a simple, accessible interface.

SmartDialer does not include advertising, third-party analytics, or user-tracking SDKs, and it does not operate a developer backend that receives users’ contacts, call logs, messages, or calendar information. Core personal information is processed on the device as described in this policy.

Parents and guardians are encouraged to review the permissions requested by SmartDialer and supervise younger users’ access to calls, messages, contacts, and calendar features as appropriate.

10. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when SmartDialer’s functionality or data-handling practices change. Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised “Last updated” date.

SmartDialer was previously developed under the CallMate name. The application name changed; this page is the canonical policy location going forward.

11. Contact

If you have questions about this policy or SmartDialer’s privacy practices, please contact the developer using the contact information provided on SmartDialer’s official Google Play Store listing.
