Privacy policy

Privacy information for the SmartDialer Android project.

Pre-release notice: SmartDialer is still in development. This page is not the final policy for a public release and is intentionally excluded from search indexing.

Privacy approach

SmartDialer is being designed with a local-first approach for core phone, messaging, contact, and productivity functionality.

Permissions

Any public release policy will list every sensitive Android permission used by the final build, explain why it is required, and describe how associated data is processed.

Data sharing and third parties

Any third-party SDKs or services included in a release build will be identified here with their purpose and relevant privacy information.

Retention and deletion

Retention and deletion behavior will be documented after the release data model and backup behavior are finalized.

Contact

A dedicated support and privacy contact will be published before a public release.
