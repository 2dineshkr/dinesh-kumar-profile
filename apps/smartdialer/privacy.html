SmartDialer Privacy Policy

SmartDialer is a phone, messaging, contacts, and productivity application designed to help users manage calls, SMS messages, contacts, reminders, tasks, and calendar-related activities.

This policy explains what information SmartDialer may access, why that access is required, and how the information is handled.

1. Information SmartDialer accesses

Depending on the features you choose to use and the permissions you grant, SmartDialer may access certain information stored on your Android device.

Contacts

Used to display contact information, identify callers, search contacts, and provide contact-related features.

Call logs

Used for recent calls, call history, summaries, and call-related functionality.

SMS & limited MMS

SMS access supports displaying, receiving, sending, and managing messages when SmartDialer is the default SMS application. MMS functionality is limited: incoming MMS may generate an unsupported-message notification, and full MMS content retrieval is not currently implemented.

Calendar

Used when you choose calendar features to display relevant information or create events requested by you.

Camera

May be used for supported functionality that requires camera access, such as supported video-call features.

Notifications

Used for calls, messages, reminders, tasks, events, and other relevant application activity.

2. How information is used

Information accessed by SmartDialer is used to provide features requested by you, including:

  • Displaying contacts and caller information
  • Displaying and managing call history
  • Initiating phone calls
  • Displaying, receiving, sending, and managing SMS messages
  • Providing reminders and notifications
  • Displaying and creating calendar events
  • Supporting call and messaging productivity features

3. Local data processing

Contact lists, SMS content, calendar records, and complete call-history datasets are primarily processed locally on your device. SmartDialer also processes call rules, tasks, notes, conferences, and message-derived intelligence locally. SMS parsing, categorization, and badges are generated on-device. SmartDialer does not operate a backend that receives these datasets. User-requested SMS delivery and sharing can transfer selected information as described below, and optional diagnostics can send limited technical information about application and call behavior to Firebase Crashlytics.

Contacts, call logs, SMS, and calendar records normally remain in the Android system providers that manage them. A calendar account configured on your device may independently synchronize calendar data according to that provider’s settings and privacy practices; SmartDialer does not perform that synchronization.

Information stored by SmartDialer

SmartDialer may store user-created tasks, notes, call-linked metadata, favorites, contact groups and rules, scheduled conferences and participant details, message marks or reactions, and limited notification state in app-private storage. SMS content may be cached temporarily on the device while an active message notification is being managed.

Notification Center

SmartDialer maintains notification-history entries and related metadata locally to organize and display information in its Notification Center. The completed data-handling review identified no separate developer-controlled upload of notification history. General crash reports may still contain information included in software-generated exception messages, as explained below.

Retention and deletion

Incoming-call attempt history is retained for approximately 31 minutes and is limited to 100 entries. Post-call state is limited to 250 entries. Other user-created information remains until you delete it using available SmartDialer controls, clear SmartDialer’s app data, or uninstall SmartDialer. Records held by Android system providers remain subject to their own deletion and retention controls.

You can revoke permissions in Android settings and remove supported messages, tasks, notes, events, and conferences using their available controls. Sensitive SmartDialer app data is excluded from Android cloud and device-transfer backups; only non-sensitive display and interaction settings are eligible for backup.

These local storage and deletion controls are separate from the handling of pending and uploaded diagnostic reports described in section 5. Clearing app data or uninstalling SmartDialer does not automatically delete reports already uploaded to Firebase.

SmartDialer does not use your contacts, call logs, SMS messages, or calendar information for advertising, marketing, or profiling. Optional technical diagnostics are described below.

4. Data sharing

SmartDialer does not sell your personal information.

Information may leave the application when you explicitly initiate an action requiring another application or service, such as opening a web link, making a phone call, composing an email or SMS, opening a map or payment link, sharing content, or opening a supported third-party messaging application. User-requested SMS delivery necessarily transfers message content through your mobile carrier to the intended recipient. User-requested sharing transfers selected content to the external application you choose. The receiving application or service handles that information under its own privacy practices.

Separately, enabling optional diagnostic reporting authorizes technical reports to be sent to Google Firebase Crashlytics, including during a later eligible application launch. You do not need to approve each report separately while reporting is enabled. SMS bodies are not intentionally included in custom diagnostics or uploaded to Firebase for analytics.

5. Optional Diagnostic Reporting

Crash reporting and call-problem reporting are optional and disabled by default, including automatic diagnostic reporting. You can enable diagnostic sharing through SmartDialer’s Diagnostics settings and continue using SmartDialer without enabling it. When enabled, SmartDialer uses Google Firebase Crashlytics to investigate crashes and technical problems. Authorized reports may upload during a later eligible application launch when network connectivity is available.

Information in diagnostic reports

Depending on the report, diagnostic information may include:

  • Crash stack traces and technical exception information
  • App version, Android version, and device information
  • Crashlytics and Firebase installation identifiers
  • Technical call-state transitions and call-failure outcomes
  • Event timestamps and elapsed timings
  • Permission and default-role states
  • Random diagnostic identifiers

General crash reports: These may contain exception messages or other technical information generated by Android and other software. Automatically generated fatal crash reports are not guaranteed to exclude every sensitive exception message.

Custom call-problem diagnostics: These are designed for technical troubleshooting and do not intentionally include contact names, phone numbers, SMS bodies, calendar descriptions, or complete call-history records. Technical call diagnostics are not a complete call-history upload.

Diagnostic controls and withdrawing consent

You can disable future reporting in Diagnostics settings. This closes SmartDialer’s authorization gate for new uploads, and the application requests deletion of pending, unsent reports through the Firebase SDK. Clearing pending reports may require an application restart or additional processing. Transmissions already started may not be recalled.

Disabling reporting does not delete reports already uploaded to Firebase. Clearing app data or uninstalling SmartDialer also does not automatically delete those uploaded reports.

Firebase processing and security

Google Firebase Crashlytics acts as a diagnostic service provider and processes diagnostic information to provide crash and troubleshooting reports. Diagnostic information is transmitted using HTTPS. This describes transmission to Firebase; it does not mean all locally stored information is encrypted. For details, see Firebase’s privacy and security documentation.

Diagnostic data retention

Firebase documents that Crashlytics keeps crash reports and associated identifiers for approximately 90 days before starting removal from its live and backup systems. Removal may take additional time and is not guaranteed to finish exactly on day 90. This retention period applies to Firebase Crashlytics reporting; local application data follows the separate storage and deletion behavior in section 3.

Deletion of uploaded reports

SmartDialer currently does not provide a verified in-app mechanism to identify and delete an individual uploaded Crashlytics report on request. Please use the developer contact information in section 11 for privacy-related inquiries. This limitation does not limit any rights you may have under applicable law.

Advertising, analytics, and tracking

SmartDialer does not include advertising SDKs. Optional Firebase Crashlytics reporting is used to diagnose crashes and technical problems. Diagnostic reporting is disabled by default and can be controlled through the application’s Diagnostics settings. SmartDialer does not use these diagnostic reports for advertising or marketing.

6. Permissions

SmartDialer requests Android permissions only when they are required for application functionality. Depending on the features you use, these permissions may include access to contacts, call logs, phone functionality, SMS, camera, notifications, and calendar information.

You can manage application permissions through your Android device settings. Disabling a permission may prevent the corresponding SmartDialer feature from working.

7. Default Phone and SMS roles

Some SmartDialer functionality requires the application to hold an appropriate Android default role, such as the default Phone or SMS application role.

SmartDialer requests permissions associated with these roles only when required for the corresponding functionality. Access to role-restricted information stops when SmartDialer no longer holds the required role.

8. Data security

SmartDialer is designed to minimize unnecessary access to personal information and to use Android’s permission and application security mechanisms to protect access to device information.

You should also keep your Android device protected and up to date to help safeguard information stored on the device.

9. Younger users and families

SmartDialer is a general-purpose dialer and productivity application intended for a broad audience aged 9 and older, including students and professionals. It is designed to make everyday communication and organization easier through a simple, accessible interface.

SmartDialer does not include advertising SDKs and does not operate a developer backend that receives users’ contacts, call logs, messages, or calendar datasets. Core personal information is primarily processed on the device. Optional Firebase Crashlytics reporting can transmit technical diagnostic information as described in section 5.

Parents and guardians are encouraged to review the permissions requested by SmartDialer and supervise younger users’ access to calls, messages, contacts, calendar features, and optional diagnostic settings as appropriate.

10. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when SmartDialer’s functionality or data-handling practices change. Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised “Last updated” date.

SmartDialer was previously developed under the CallMate name. The application name changed; this page is the canonical policy location going forward.

11. Contact

If you have questions about this policy or SmartDialer’s privacy practices, please contact the developer using the contact information provided on SmartDialer’s official Google Play Store listing.
